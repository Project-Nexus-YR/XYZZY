# MultiAI

## Register

product

## Users

AI-native technical teams of three to five people making consequential technical decisions together. They work on desktop displays, often across multiple authenticated browser tabs, and need to move quickly between human conversation, parallel specialist analysis, deliberate selection, synthesis, and evidence inspection.

## Product Purpose

MultiAI is a multiplayer AI workspace for shared technical decision-making. Its core loop is Workspace to Channel to Human conversation to AI Branch to Agent Runs to Individual Outputs to Select or Exclude to Synthesis to Artifact. Success means that a new participant can understand and manually exercise this loop without knowing the backend architecture, while every selected result remains inspectable, durable, permission-aware, and attributable to its exact inputs.

## Brand Personality

Focused, rigorous, collaborative. The product should feel calm under pressure, fast to scan, and trustworthy enough for serious team decisions. Its voice is direct and operational, with human-readable explanations instead of implementation terminology.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a SaaS landing page, admin dashboard, collection of floating cards, generic ChatGPT clone, purple-gradient AI product, or prototype/debug console. Avoid oversized headings, decorative gradients, glassmorphism, excessive whitespace, gratuitous animation, emoji as primary icons, and raw backend identifiers in primary workflows.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the channel conversation primary; reveal AI work and evidence in context without displacing collaboration.
2. Make branches coherent and inspectable: strategy, participating agents, run state, review state, and synthesis readiness must be obvious at a glance.
3. Prefer progressive disclosure: show decisions and useful provenance first, then exact evidence and provider details on demand.
4. Design for fast multiplayer testing: identity, location, presence, synchronization, permissions, and current turn ownership remain legible.
5. Use established desktop application patterns when they reduce cognitive load, while preserving MultiAI's identity around governed multiplayer AI.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, focus visibility, semantics, keyboard operation, reduced motion, and status communication that does not rely on color alone. Preserve readable density, minimum practical hit targets, and a coherent narrow-screen fallback centered on the conversation.
